#### Infuse your AI with Atlassian context

access information spanning people, services, knowledge, and work right
inside your LLMs.

#### Stay in the flow

Whether you need context from Atlassian in Claude, or guidance on how to

Approach challenges at scale, with the ability to summarize work
across Atlassian as well as create new Confluence pages or Jira work

## Trusted access, protected data

Connect your AI clients through our official remote MCP server, secured
with OAuth authentication and granular permission controls. Atlassian
offers a default list of supported AI domains, and admins can add or
block domains to create a trusted list that matches their organization’s
security policies.

## Let your agents work together

Connect external AI agents to Rovo via the Atlassian Agent2Agent (A2A)
Gateway. Instead of managing individual steps, give Rovo a complete goal
— search, summarize, and create across Jira and Confluence.

Works with A2A-compatible clients like Google Gemini. One Agent Card and
OAuth connect you, no custom integration needed. Every action runs
within the user’s permissions and your org’s admin controls.

[Get
started](https://support.atlassian.com/security-and-access-policies/docs/manage-a2a-connections-in-admin-hub/)
